# Aegean Perennial Cropping

## The setting

The Aegean region around Izmir and Manisa has a Mediterranean climate with
roughly 700 mm of rainfall concentrated in winter and a mean temperature near
17.5 C. Soils are predominantly sandy loams with calcareous areas, generally
well drained.

The combination of mild wet winters and hot dry summers suits perennial tree and
vine crops, and the region is the country's centre for olive and table grape
production, with cotton grown on the irrigated lowlands.

## Olive

Olive tolerates the calcareous soils and high pH that limit other crops, and its
modest water requirement of around 450 mm suits the rainfall pattern. The dominant
pest is the olive fruit fly, which lays into developing fruit from late summer
into autumn; infested fruit yields oil with elevated acidity and is downgraded or
rejected.

Pheromone mass trapping is the preferred control here because the fly has a single
dominant host, limited immigration where whole blocks are covered, and a mating
biology that suits trap-out. Spinosad applied as a protein bait is the standard
supplementary treatment and is permitted in organic production.

Verticillium wilt also affects olive, arriving in orchards planted on land
previously cropped with susceptible hosts such as cotton.

## Grape

Grapevine faces two mildews that behave quite differently. Powdery mildew does not
require free water and develops through the dry summer; wettable sulfur is the
classic control and remains effective after more than a century, because its
multi-site action gives the pathogen no simple route to resistance. Downy mildew
does require leaf wetness and appears in spring after rain; copper hydroxide is the
standard organic-compatible option.

## Export exposure

Aegean olives and table grapes are heavily export oriented, so EU maximum residue
levels under Regulation (EC) No 396/2005 govern product choice in practice, and
the region has a substantial certified organic sector operating under Regulation
(EU) 2018/848.
