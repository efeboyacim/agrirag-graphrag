# Organic Certification

## The framework

Organic production in the European Union is governed by Regulation (EU) 2018/848,
which defines permitted inputs, conversion periods, record-keeping obligations and
labelling rules. Turkey operates a parallel national framework under Organic
Farming Law No. 5262, administered by the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry.

Certification is a process audit, not a product test. An inspector verifies that
the production method complied with the rules; residue testing supplements that
but does not replace it.

## Permitted inputs

The regulation works from a positive list. Substances not on it may not be used,
which reverses the usual conventional logic where anything authorised is
available.

Among the inputs relevant to the crops covered here, Bacillus thuringiensis,
spinosad, neem-derived azadirachtin, Trichoderma-based biofungicides, copper
hydroxide, wettable sulfur, compost and agricultural gypsum are all acceptable in
certified organic production. Synthetic pyrethroids such as deltamethrin and
lambda-cyhalothrin, organophosphates such as chlorpyrifos, and synthetic
fungicides such as tebuconazole and azoxystrobin are not.

Copper deserves a caveat: it is permitted but subject to cumulative annual load
limits, because it accumulates in soil and does not degrade.

## Conversion

Land must be managed organically for a conversion period - commonly two years for
annual crops and three for perennials - before the harvest can be sold as organic.
The crop grown during conversion is managed to organic rules but sold
conventionally, which is the main financial obstacle to entry.

## A common misconception

Organic approval and low toxicity are not the same thing, and neither implies a
short pre-harvest interval. Copper and sulfur are permitted organic fungicides
with real environmental and handling considerations. The regulation asks whether a
substance is of natural origin and acceptable within the system's principles, not
whether it is harmless.
