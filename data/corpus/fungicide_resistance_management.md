# Fungicide Resistance Management

## How resistance develops

A fungal population contains rare individuals with reduced sensitivity to a given
mode of action before that product is ever used. Repeated application kills the
sensitive majority and leaves the insensitive minority to reproduce. Within a few
seasons of sole reliance, the resistant subpopulation dominates and the product
stops working in the field while still working in the laboratory assay.

The speed depends on the pathogen's reproductive rate and the genetics of the
resistance. Pathogens producing many generations per season under high selection
pressure shift fastest.

## Modes of action at different risk

Strobilurins, of which azoxystrobin is the common example, inhibit a single
respiration site. Resistance arises from a single point mutation and is
effectively all-or-nothing, making this group high risk. Zymoseptoria tritici
populations across Europe have largely lost sensitivity to this group.

Triazoles such as tebuconazole inhibit sterol biosynthesis. Resistance here builds
gradually through the accumulation of several mutations, so sensitivity erodes
over years rather than collapsing in one season. Medium risk.

Multi-site protectants such as copper hydroxide and elemental sulfur interfere
with many cellular targets at once. A pathogen would need simultaneous changes at
several sites, which is why these old materials have retained their effectiveness
for a century.

## Practical rules

Alternate modes of action rather than products; two trade names sharing an active
substance provide no alternation at all. Limit the number of applications of any
single high-risk group per season, commonly to two. Where a mixture is used,
ensure the partner is genuinely effective on its own against the target, since a
weak partner provides no protection to the strong one.

Above all, reduce the number of sprays needed in the first place. A resistant
cultivar lowers selection pressure more reliably than any spray programme design,
because the applications it prevents impose no selection at all.
