# Biological Control Agents

## Categories

Microbial agents are living organisms applied like a pesticide. Bacillus
thuringiensis produces crystal proteins that dissolve in the alkaline larval
midgut of susceptible insects and destroy the gut lining; the kurstaki subspecies
is specific to lepidopteran larvae. Trichoderma harzianum colonises the root zone
and suppresses soilborne pathogens through competition, parasitism and induced
plant resistance.

Fermentation-derived products are not living but originate biologically. Spinosad
comes from the soil actinomycete Saccharopolyspora spinosa and acts on insect
nicotinic acetylcholine receptors at a site unrelated to that of neonicotinoids.

Botanical extracts such as neem-derived azadirachtin act mainly as growth
regulators and antifeedants rather than fast-acting toxicants.

## Strengths

Selectivity is the main one. Bt affects only larvae that ingest it and leaves
predators, parasitoids and pollinators untouched, so the field retains its
natural enemy population.

Short pre-harvest intervals follow from low mammalian toxicity, which makes these
materials the practical choice for late-season infestations regardless of whether
the grower is certified organic.

Most are also permitted under organic regulations, which matters for
export-oriented production.

## Limitations

Speed is the recurring disappointment. Bt-killed larvae stop feeding within hours
but take days to die, which reads as failure to an operator expecting a
pyrethroid's knockdown. Coverage must be thorough because the larva has to eat the
material, and it degrades under ultraviolet light within days, so timing against
egg hatch matters far more than it does for a persistent product.

Efficacy also falls as larvae grow. Applications targeted at early instars work;
the same product against large larvae already inside a boll or stem does not.

## Resistance is still possible

Selectivity does not confer immunity from resistance. Spinosad resistance has
been documented in several pest populations under intensive use, and the same
alternation discipline that applies to synthetic products applies here.
